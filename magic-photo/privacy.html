Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use Magic Photo AI.

Disclaimer: This app uses AI-generated image transformations for entertainment and creative purposes. The generated images may appear realistic or misleading. Users are solely responsible for how they use, share, or distribute generated content. Please use the app responsibly and respect the privacy and consent of others.

1. Information We Collect

We collect or receive the following types of information:

  • Photos / images you select: The image you choose as source material for an AI edit (for example from your camera or photo library)
  • Related inputs you provide: Optional text or settings you enter for a generation (for example a theme or caption)
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers used for authentication and security
  • Usage data: How you interact with the App, features used, and preferences
  • Purchase information: Transaction history for subscriptions and credits processed through Apple

Photos are collected when you choose to generate or regenerate an AI result. We ask for your permission in the App before your photo is first sent for AI processing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Send your selected photo (and related inputs) to create the AI-edited image you requested
  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Process transactions and manage your account
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Communicate with you about updates and support

3. Photo Processing & Third-Party AI

When you generate an AI result:

  • Your selected photo is uploaded to our servers as source material for the edit
  • We then transmit that photo (and related generation inputs) to third-party AI image services that manipulate or transform the image to produce your result
  • Those providers process the content on our behalf to fulfill your request; the specific AI engine may vary by feature
  • We do not sell your photos and do not use them for third-party advertising
  • We do not permanently store your original photos as a user library; generation artifacts may be held temporarily to deliver the result and operate the service
  • Generated images are delivered to your device and may be temporarily cached

We require third parties that process this data for us to protect it with safeguards appropriate to the service they provide.

4. Data Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Third-party AI image providers: Your selected photo and related inputs, solely to generate the edited image you requested
  • Cloud / infrastructure providers: To host uploads and deliver results securely
  • Payment Processors: To handle subscriptions via Apple's App Store
  • Analytics Services: To understand App usage (where used)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication using Apple's App Attest
  • Regular security audits and updates

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our services. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting our support team.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of certain data collection

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the App and updating the "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, please contact us through the App's support feature.
