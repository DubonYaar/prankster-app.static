What is Magic Photo AI?

Magic Photo AI is an iOS app for anyone who wants more from their camera roll. Pick a photo, choose a look, and let AI deliver high-quality results you can save and share.

Whether you are restoring an old picture, trying a creative style, or polishing a portrait, Magic Photo AI keeps the focus on great results—without complicated editing tools.
